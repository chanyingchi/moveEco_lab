Hi! I am an ecologist with a background in behavioural ecology, movement ecology, birds and coastal systems. My current research centers around the theme of movement ecology and its applications to inform wildlife conservation and management, with a focus on birds and Asia. I am now an assistant professor at the Asian School of the Environment, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. Much of my research has been on migratory shorebirds in the East Asian-Australasian Flyway, and I worked on migration and natal dispersal of raptors (Red Kites and Little Owls) during my postdoc. 

Besides research, I enjoy many outdoor activities like hiking and kayaking, or just chilling in nature - especially with friends and family.
