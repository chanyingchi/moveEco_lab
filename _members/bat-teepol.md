Hi! I am a PhD student at NTU’s Asian School of the Environment. For my PhD, my research will focus on threatened migratory shorebirds in Southeast Asia, with a particular interest in understanding their movements, habitat use and dependence on coastal wetlands. Using tracking alongside long-term monitoring and spatial data, I want to examine how shorebirds use these habitats, how they are changing and what this means for their conservation. Outside of birds, I enjoy herping, reading or watching murder mysteries and thinking about my cats back in Sarawak.
